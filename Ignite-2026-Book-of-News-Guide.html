Azure

Built from chip to agent

AI is in production, and agents need the whole stack. Azure now brings silicon, infrastructure, data and operations together as one system. Cobalt 200 VMs, Azure HorizonDB, Rayfin and Fabric apps are generally available, and new previews bring Foundry models and agents to Azure Kubernetes Service.

Good first stepRunning PostgreSQL or planning a migration? Look at Azure HorizonDB and Azure Catalyst first.

1.1Generally available

Azure Chaos Studio Workspaces & AKS extension

Break things on purpose, safely, to prove your apps can recover.

  • Workspaces organize resilience tests with reusable disruption scenarios, such as losing an availability zone or failing over a database.
  • A new AKS extension injects controlled faults into Kubernetes pods and needs fewer permissions to run.

Why it mattersYou find the weak spots in a controlled test instead of during a real outage.

Expanded Azure confidential computing

Protects sensitive data while it is being processed, as well as when it is stored or moving.

  • VR72 multi-GPU confidential computing opens a path to protect large models that span more than one GPU.
  • Azure Confidential Enclaves and AKS Kata CoCo containers shrink what you have to trust to a small, verified environment.
  • Azure Confidential Clean Room lets several parties analyze shared data without exposing each other's raw data.

Why it mattersLets you use data for AI and partner work that was once too sensitive to touch.

1.3Generally available

Azure Cobalt 200 virtual machines

Microsoft's own Arm-based CPU is now generally available and tuned for the CPU-heavy work agents do.

  • Up to 50% better CPU performance than Cobalt 100.
  • Coming to 24 Azure regions by the end of the year, with a consistent-speed or boost mode for each VM.
  • New VM types: Mpsv4 for large in-memory databases and Lpsv5 with up to 23 TB of built-in storage.

Why it mattersAgents do much of their work on general-purpose CPUs, outside the model. Faster CPUs finish tasks sooner and get more done per machine.

Microsoft Foundry

Agents that keep getting better

Foundry now uses live production signals to suggest and test improvements to every layer of an AI system: an agent's setup, its evaluations and even the model behind it. New Microsoft AI (MAI) and partner models expand the choice.

Good first stepDefine what a good result looks like for one agent, run multi-turn evaluations, then let agent optimizer propose improvements.
In this chapterContinuous improvement 1New models 2

Continuous improvement1 announcement

2.1Generally availablePreview

Continuous improvement in Foundry

A learning loop that turns what happens in production into better agents.

  • GA: multi-turn evaluation of whole conversations, and an insights feature that groups recurring failures such as hallucinations and token spikes.
  • GA: agent optimizer proposes changes and tests them against your evaluations. You choose what ships.
  • Preview: human annotation for golden datasets, Managed Training, and Programmed Training (fine-tuning and reinforcement learning on open-weight models).

Why it mattersEach improvement you deploy becomes the new baseline. You can raise quality or cut cost and still catch regressions.

New models2 announcements

2.2Generally available

Microsoft-Decision-1.1

A fast, low-cost model built for decisions such as routing, classification, prioritization and verification.

  • Picks from the actions you allow and returns calibrated confidence scores, so your app can hold back when it's unsure.
  • Scores 68 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 (6 points higher than v1) and runs more than twice as fast at the same price. It now accepts images.
  • Available in Foundry, OpenRouter and Vercel. It already routes requests in Copilot and powers Auto in Cowork.

Why it mattersCan handle uncertain routing and classification while reserving larger models for complex reasoning. Keep deterministic policy and authorization checks outside model decisions.

  • Security: MAI-Cyber-2-Flash is in preview inside Codename MDASH. Routing: the model router now covers the top 50 Foundry models and works with AI Gateway.
