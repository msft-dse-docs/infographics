0
Awareness

Executive Briefing

Build trust. Set the agenda.
⏱ ½ day 👥 Execs, sponsors 📍 Experience Center One · Regional Experience Centers

A strategic, curated conversation to show what's possible, frame Microsoft's POV on your industry, and align leadership on why now. Not a demo tour — a directed, decision-ready dialogue.

What you get
  • Industry-specific Microsoft POV
  • AI trends and peer benchmarks
  • Executive-level Q&A with leaders
  • Bias to act and named next step
You bring
  • C-suite and VP-level sponsors
  • Top 2–3 strategic questions
  • A willingness to challenge and be challenged
You leave with
  • Shared language around the opportunity
  • Agreement on whether to proceed
  • A name, a date, and a phase for next step
Ideal ifLeadership is aware of AI pressure but hasn't committed. You need inspiration and alignment before you can justify scoped investment.
1
Discovery

Business Envisioning

Explore wide. Narrow with intent.
⏱ 1 day 👥 Business + IT, mixed roles 📍 Microsoft Innovation Hub

A facilitated human-centered design thinking workshop. Your Technical Architect guides your team through divergent exploration, then convergent prioritization — ending with a ranked shortlist of use cases and a clear next move.

What you get
  • Design thinking workshop (full day)
  • Persona and journey mapping
  • Opportunity framing and prioritization
  • Shortlist of 2–4 use cases ranked by value × feasibility
You bring
  • 6–12 cross-functional participants
  • A broad business theme, not a solution
  • Authority to pick winners by end of day
You leave with
  • Prioritized use case(s) with articulated value
  • A persona-driven problem statement per use case
  • Defined next step — usually Solution Envisioning
Ideal ifYou know you want to do "something with AI" in a domain, but haven't picked the specific use case or aligned stakeholders on why it matters.

Direction

Solution Envisioning

Agree on the "how."
⏱ 1 day 👥 Business owners + tech leads 📍 Microsoft Innovation Hub · Envisioning Theater

A strategic business-and-technical day: discovery in the morning, immersive demos in the envisioning theater, then use-case-to-solution mapping after lunch. You leave with a picture of how Microsoft + partners can actually deliver the outcome.

What you get
  • Deep business + technical discovery
  • Envisioning theater: immersive solution tours
  • Partner / ISV fit analysis
  • Actionable solution direction
You bring
  • A named use case and owner
  • Business goals and success metrics
  • Technical constraints (data, regulation, existing stack)
You leave with
  • Shared solution vision across biz + IT
  • Microsoft + partner mapping for your scenario
  • Inputs ready for Architecture Design
Ideal ifYou've picked the use case and need to align business + IT on the shape of the solution before committing architecture or vendor decisions.

Blueprint

Architecture Design

Build confidence. De-risk the decision.
⏱ 1–2 days (custom) 👥 CTOs, enterprise architects, senior devs 📍 Microsoft Innovation Hub

A custom technical session where Microsoft architects synthesize your business and technical requirements into a high-level architecture — aligned to reference architectures, with preferred-practice guidance and explicit risk analysis.

What you get
  • Target architecture diagram
  • Alignment to Microsoft reference architectures
  • Preferred-practice guidance
  • Risk register + mitigation recommendations
You bring
  • Agreed solution scope and non-functional reqs
  • Current-state architecture artifacts
  • Your architect(s), CTO, and tech decision-makers
You leave with
  • Architecture-ready brief for your delivery team
  • Go/no-go signal on technical feasibility
  • Prototype scope if further validation is needed
Ideal ifThe business case and solution shape are agreed, and now you need a credible, risk-aware technical blueprint before committing delivery budget.
4
Proof

Rapid Prototype

Prove the hard part. Fast.
⏱ Multi-week, co-delivered 👥 Your engineers + Microsoft architects 📍 Hub dev suite · Private, preconfigured

An in-depth co-development engagement. Microsoft architects work shoulder-to-shoulder with your technical staff in a private, fully-loaded Hub dev suite — building a working prototype that proves the riskiest technical capability.

What you get
  • Working prototype on your data / scenario
  • Hands-on knowledge transfer
  • Targeted demos and training
  • Private, preconfigured dev suite
You bring
  • A sharply scoped technical hypothesis to test
  • Dedicated engineers (not part-time)
  • Realistic test data and access to systems
You leave with
  • Code, not slides
  • Validated technical feasibility
  • An internal team that has built it once
Ideal ifYou're ready to commit — but the single biggest risk is a technical unknown. A prototype retires that risk in weeks, not quarters.

Proven sequences

How customers actually move through the framework.

You rarely do just one phase. Most successful Hub engagements string together 2–3 phases over 4–12 weeks. Three common paths:

The curious executive

"We know AI matters. We don't know where to start."

State: exec-led curiosity, no picked use case, mixed internal opinions.

Executive Briefing → Business Envisioning → Solution Envisioning

→ From "should we?" to a funded, scoped pilot in under 8 weeks.

The scoped builder

"We picked the use case. Now we need to build it right."

State: business case approved, solution direction emerging, technical leadership engaged.

Solution Envisioning → Architecture Design → Rapid Prototype

→ From approved idea to working code on your data in 6–10 weeks.

The de-risker

"One technical unknown is blocking a major commitment."

State: leadership is ready, architecture is drafted, but a hard technical question is creating risk.

Architecture Design → Rapid Prototype

→ Binary "works / doesn't" evidence before the next funding gate.

Before you request a visit

Common questions, honest answers.

Do I have to start at phase 0 and go in order?
No. The framework is designed so any phase is a valid entry point. Starting at Solution Envisioning or Architecture Design is common when the use case is already picked. The only rule: pick the phase that matches your current state, not the one that looks most impressive.
How much does a Hub engagement cost?
Innovation Hub engagements are delivered by invitation to strategic Microsoft customers and are funded by Microsoft for qualified scenarios. Your Microsoft account team can confirm eligibility and scheduling. Travel and your own team's time are yours to plan for.
Who needs to attend from my side?
It depends on the phase. Executive Briefing → C-suite and VPs. Business Envisioning → cross-functional (biz + IT), 6–12 people. Solution Envisioning → business owners + tech leads. Architecture Design → CTOs, enterprise architects, senior engineers. Rapid Prototype → dedicated engineers, not part-time observers. Right people at the right phase is the single biggest driver of value.
Can I combine phases in a single visit?
Sometimes — for example, an Executive Briefing in the morning followed by Business Envisioning the next day, or Solution Envisioning leading into an Architecture Design session. Your Technical Architect will scope this with you. Stacking too many phases into one compressed visit usually reduces value; each phase has its own cadence for a reason.
What if we don't know which phase we're in?
Use the selector at the top of this page, then talk to your Microsoft account team. They'll run a short readiness conversation with you to confirm — it's a ~30-minute pre-call, not another workshop. Getting the phase right is their job, not yours.
Do I get a deliverable I can take back to my team?
Yes. Every phase produces tangible outputs — prioritized use cases, solution maps, architecture diagrams, risk registers, or working prototype code. Hub engagements are designed to be decision-ready, not conversation-only.
Is the Innovation Hub the same as a Microsoft Technology Center?
Yes — the Microsoft Innovation Hub is the new name for what used to be called the Microsoft Technology Center (MTC) network. Same mission, refreshed spaces, expanded AI focus. There are roughly 50 locations worldwide.

Ready to pick a phase?

Talk to your Microsoft account team — they'll confirm your phase, scope the engagement, and schedule your day at the nearest Innovation Hub. Go in knowing what you want; walk out with what you need.
